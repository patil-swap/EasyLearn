Arrival and Denial
Upon arriving on Arrakis, Paul is a product of his training—Bene Gesserit, Mentat, and Atreides. He experiences prescient visions but views them as a burden or an anomaly rather than destiny. His primary focus is on survival and political positioning following the Harkonnen betrayal.

The Desert Awakening
Fleeing into the deep desert with Jessica forces his exposure to raw spice. This acts as a catalyst, violently expanding his prescience. During this phase, he attempts to resist the "terrible purpose" (the jihad) he sees in his visions, trying to find a path that avoids galactic holy war while still avenging his father.

Acceptance and the Water of Life
To secure the absolute loyalty of the Fremen and to unlock the final barrier of his prescient vision, Paul chooses to undergo the spice agony, drinking the Water of Life. Surviving this confirms his status as the Kwisatz Haderach. He no longer fights the current of time but weaponizes it, accepting that to defeat the Emperor and the Guild, he must unleash the religious fervor he previously feared.
